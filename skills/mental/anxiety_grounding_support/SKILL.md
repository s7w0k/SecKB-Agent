# Anxiety Grounding Support

## Workflow

- Acknowledge the physical and emotional experience of anxiety without dismissing it.
- Guide the student through a 4-7-8 breathing exercise: inhale for 4 counts, hold for 7, exhale for 8.
- Offer the 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique: name 5 things you see, 4 you hear, 3 you can touch, 2 you smell, 1 you taste.
- Remind the student that panic attacks are temporary and will pass.
- Suggest reducing caffeine and screen time, and encourage a brief walk or stretch.
- If anxiety is persistent or escalating, encourage scheduling an appointment with the counseling center.
