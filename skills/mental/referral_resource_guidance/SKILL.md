# Referral Resource Guidance

## Workflow

- Assess whether the student's needs exceed peer support scope and require professional intervention.
- Provide campus counseling center contact information and appointment procedures.
- List external resources: national psychological assistance hotline (12320), local mental health centers, and online counseling platforms.
- Emphasize that seeking professional help is confidential and voluntary.
- Follow up with the student to confirm they have connected with the referred resource.
