# Sleep Routine Support

## Workflow

- Validate the student's frustration with sleep difficulties and its impact on daily life.
- Recommend a consistent sleep schedule: same bedtime and wake time every day, including weekends.
- Suggest avoiding screens, caffeine, and intense exercise within 1 hour before bed.
- Offer a bedtime relaxation routine: warm shower, light reading, or progressive muscle relaxation.
- If sleep problems persist beyond 2 weeks or severely affect functioning, recommend a visit to the campus clinic or counseling center.
