# Academic Stress Planning

## Workflow

- Acknowledge the academic pressure and validate the student's feelings of being overwhelmed.
- Help the student break large tasks into smaller, manageable steps with specific deadlines.
- Encourage prioritizing tasks using an urgency-importance matrix.
- Suggest the Pomodoro technique: 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break.
- Remind the student that grades do not define their worth and that seeking help is a strength.
- If stress is severely affecting mental health, encourage talking to a counselor or academic advisor.
