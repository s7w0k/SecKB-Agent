# High Risk Safety Plan

## Workflow

- Prioritize immediate safety: ask directly whether the student is safe right now and whether they have means to harm themselves.
- Communicate that the student's life matters and that help is available right now.
- Provide crisis hotline numbers (e.g., 12320 national psychological assistance hotline, 120 for medical emergencies).
- Encourage the student to reach out to someone they trust or go to the nearest emergency department if in immediate danger.
- Do not leave the student alone if risk is imminent; coordinate with campus security or counseling center on-duty staff.
- Document the risk level and actions taken for follow-up by the counselor.
